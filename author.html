Biography

A life spent in the field

Howard Williams is a retired Seventh-day Adventist pastor whose ministry has taken him across Alaska, Idaho, Bolivia, and the Philippines, where he served as a missionary, mission pilot, church planter, and director of a home for street boys. He earned his religion degree at Andrews University, where he also trained as a pilot and aircraft mechanic. He and his wife, Charleen, now reside in Oregon.
