Biography

A life spent in the field

Howard Williams is a retired Seventh-day Adventist pastor whose ministry has taken him across Alaska, Idaho, Bolivia, and the Philippines, where he served as a missionary, mission pilot, church planter, and director of a home for street boys. A fifth-generation Adventist, he trained as a pilot and aircraft mechanic at Andrews University. He and his wife, Charleen, now reside in Oregon, devoted to one conviction: that the heart of the gospel is Christ dwelling within the believer.
