Adam’s Lost Dominion

And God’s Plan to Restore It

by Howard Williams

When Adam fell, he surrendered more than innocence — he forfeited a kingdom, and with it the indwelling presence of God that made humanity fully alive. In these pages, Pastor Howard Williams traces the grand narrative of salvation from Eden to the empty tomb, recovering a truth the church has too often missed: that Christ did not merely forgive sin, but won back the kingdom Adam lost.

  • Why the everlasting gospel must come before the warnings of the end
  • How the fall of Adam shifted the dominion of this world — and how Christ won it back
  • What truly happens at conversion, when a new heart and a new nature are given
  • Justification as your title to heaven, sanctification as your fitness for it
  • Assurance that rests not on your performance, but on the One who holds you fast

About the author

Howard Williams

Howard Williams is a retired Seventh-day Adventist pastor whose ministry has taken him across Alaska, Idaho, Bolivia, and the Philippines, where he served as a missionary, mission pilot, church planter, and director of a home for street boys. A fifth-generation Adventist, he trained as a pilot and aircraft mechanic at Andrews University. He and his wife, Charleen, now reside in Oregon, devoted to one conviction: that the heart of the gospel is Christ dwelling within the believer.
